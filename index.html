Welcome

A Spiritual Home for Every Devotee

Hari Om Shri Radha Krishna Mandir — known locally as the Hayward Hindu Temple — is a place of prayer, peace, and togetherness. With Shri Radha Krishna as our presiding deities, the mandir serves the wider Hindu community of Hayward and the East Bay.

Our mission is simple: to keep the lamp of devotion burning through daily worship, scripture, bhajans, and seva — and to offer families a place where our traditions are lived, celebrated, and passed on.

“Whoever offers Me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water — that offering of love I accept.”

— Bhagavad Gita 9.26
