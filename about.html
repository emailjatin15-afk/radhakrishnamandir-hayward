Presiding Deities

Divine Presence at the Mandir

Main Deity

Shri Radha Krishna

Shri Krishna, the beloved of Vrindavan, and Shri Radha, the embodiment of pure devotion, are the heart of our mandir. Together they represent divine love — the eternal bond between the soul and the Supreme.

Devotees come for darshan, to offer flowers and prasad, to sing their names in kirtan, and to find peace in their presence. Special celebrations are held on Janmashtami, Radha Ashtami, Holi, and Govardhan Puja.

Lord Shiva

Mahadev, the auspicious one — worshipped with abhishek, especially on Mondays and Maha Shivratri.

Shri Hanuman

The devoted servant of Shri Ram, embodiment of strength and courage — honored with Hanuman Chalisa.

Durga Mata

The Divine Mother who protects her devotees — celebrated with great joy during Navratri.

Shri Ganesh

Remover of obstacles, invoked at the start of every puja and new beginning.

Our Values & Community

Bhakti

Loving devotion to the Divine, expressed through worship, kirtan, and prayer.

Seva

Selfless service to the temple and to others — from preparing prasad to helping neighbors.

Sanskriti

Preserving scripture, language, music, and festivals so our heritage lives on in our children.

Sangha

A warm, inclusive community where every family and visitor feels at home.
