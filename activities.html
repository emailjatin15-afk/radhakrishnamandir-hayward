Every Day

Daily Darshan & Aarti

The mandir is open every day from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM for darshan. Visitors are welcome to join aarti, offer prayers, and sit in quiet reflection.

Visiting etiquette

Please remove shoes before entering the prayer hall, dress modestly, and keep phones on silent during aarti.
